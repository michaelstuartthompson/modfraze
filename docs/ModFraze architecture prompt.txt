ModFraze is a maximalist abstract graphic design brand created by artist, muralist, AI/tech generalist, and author Michael Stuart Thompson.

ModFraze sells one-of-a-kind abstract graphic designs digitally and on drop-shipped merchandise. The brand must support revenue generation, audience growth, repeatable design-to-market workflows, and portfolio/resume-building evidence of technical skill.

This is not only an art shop. It is also a visible proof-of-work system showing Michael’s ability to combine art, AI, automation, e-commerce, analytics, content systems, and startup execution.

ModFraze must connect to the larger MichaelStuartThompson.com ecosystem, which includes:

* original painting and mural work
* AI/tech generalist services
* writing/memoir projects
* digital products
* creative consulting
* portfolio/resume material

The colors, fonts, landing page, and broader brand design are still in progress. Focus on the **bootstrapping architecture and MVP operating system**, not final visual branding.
